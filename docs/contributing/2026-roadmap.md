# 2026 Roadmap

The trajectory for **B** in April and May of 2026 is about transitioning from a technical repo to a living simulation. We want to move beyond templates and build an intuitive environment where lore and mechanics blend together.

---

## Part One: Foundations (Current State)

The archive is currently a structural framework designed for rapid expansion.

* **Documentation Core**: The primary wiki repo is live, laying out the rules for **Player Styles**, **Heritage**, and **Integrity**.
* **Artistic Infrastructure**: Concept artwork templates and the master **GIMP** power icon generator are operational for cohesive asset creation.
* **Scripting Base**: Initial **DenizenScript** accessories and **AHK 2.0** macros for network coordination are in development.

---

## Part Two: Issue 1 - The Game

✨ **Current progress!** ✨

This phase represents the archive's entry-level data. It needs to be dense, fruitful, and completely working before we move forward.

#### Lore & Narrative
* **The Lore**: Build a comprehensive history of the **Galactic Federation of B** and its scientific mission.
* **The Cities**: Detail the atmosphere and operational hazards of **Atlas** and **Lemon City**.
* **Lore Integration**: Flavor every mechanic page with the gritty 90s comic aesthetic - move away from dry technical manuals.

#### Systems Documentation
* **The Mechanic Pages**: Finalize or mostly complete detailed docs for **Melee**, **Ranged**, **Support**, and **Neural** powerset pools.
* **Operational Layout**: Map out the physical interface and how players interact with world objects, shops, and the environment.
* **The Lifecycle**: Outline the transition from manifestation to growth to **Roster Management: Total Death** stasis.

#### Organic Systems Discovery
* **Tutorial Missions**: Design missions that teach gameplay through environmental puzzles and combat encounters.
* **The "Hands-Off" Approach**: We prefer organic discovery over intrusive popups or UI hand-holding. If a mechanic isn't immediately obvious, it should be learned by observing the simulation's behavior, completing specific mission objectives, or through experimentation.

---

## Part Three: Operational Systems (The Next Step)

Once the documentation for **Issue 1** is verified as complete, development will shift toward the primary user interfaces.

* **Account System**: Establishing the persistent database for player rosters and global **Total Level** tracking.
* **Login Interface**: Developing a secure, intuitive login portal that matches the dark/neon digital aesthetic.
* **Player Creation Interface**: A dedicated UI for navigating the **Bio-Synthetic Scale** and selecting initial **Archetypes** and **Heritages**.
