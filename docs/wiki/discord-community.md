# Discord Community

The **B** network is a collaborative collective of developers, artists, and lore-keepers. Our Discord is the primary hub for real-time coordination, asset vetting, and technical discussion before data is finalized in the archive.

---

## Join the Server

![](/../assets/discord/800x400_2.png)

[**Join the B Discord Server**](https://discord.gg/mpxGDSJkkW)

---

## Sector Directory

The Discord cluster is organized into specific intuitive channels to facilitate development and community interaction.

* **# 📕 information**: General orientation and welcoming.
* **# 🔹 text-chat**: Casual discussion and general chatter.
* **# 📄 development & project-chatter**: The primary workspace for contributors to coordinate on scripts lore, and assets.
* **# 🌐 game-chat**: A live relay for in-game chatter from play.behr.dev.
* **# 🤖 bot-spam**: High-volume automated interaction and bot testing.
* **# 📕 news-feed**: Centralized feeds for changelogs, project updates, and crossposts.
