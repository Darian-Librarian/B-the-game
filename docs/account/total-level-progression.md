# Level Progression

In **B**, individual growth feeds a larger machine. Your **Total Level** is the sum of all levels across your entire roster. This account-wide metric multiplies your **Efficiency** and sets the baseline power for every new entity you acquire or build.

---

## Collective Strength

Traditional games focus on one character. In **B**, your strength comes from the experience and **Skill Memory** of your entire network. As your **Total Level** rises, the **B Multiplier** gives even your lowest-level players a passive stat floor.

### Level Contribution Logic

Not all entities contribute equally to the **Total Level** - this prevents unit padding exploits.

* **Standard and Ironman Players**: Contribute **100%** of their level.
* **Neural Players**: Diminishing returns as they grow - the bigger and stronger they get, the less they contribute to the total.
* **Minions**: Temporary entities like illusions, pets, or decoys contribute **zero**.

---

## Infinite Advancement

Beyond the standard level caps, **B** usess an infinite advancement system. Once a player reaches their maximum individual level, experience earned feeds into a pool of **Enhancement Points**.Â 

These points can be spent to infinitely enhance specific stats, powers, and abilities across your entire player roster. This ensures that even after hitting the primary milestones, your account continues to grow in power through specialized refinements in speed, damage, or elemental affinity.

---

## Impact of Player Loss

Because the **Total Level** is a sum of your player roster, the death of a permanent player has immediate consequences:

* **Level Reduction**: Your Total Level drops by the amount held by the deceased player, adjusted for their contribution type.
* **Efficiency Dip**: Passive bonuses and stat floors are recalculated instantly, potentially weakening surviving players.
* **Roster Management: Total Death**: If your Total Level hits zero due to a full roster wipe, this state prevents any new progress for 24 hours from the last creation timestamp.
