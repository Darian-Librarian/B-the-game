# Heritage & Body Types

The **Heritage** is your Soul Architecture, while the **Body Type** is your current physical state. Every player exists as a manifestation of their starting heritage, which determines how they initially interact with the **Integrity Axis**.

---

## Heritage Classifications

Heritage determines your baseline traits and affinity groups. Whether you were **Manifested** via natural mutation or **Created** by **The Galactic Federation of B**, your architecture is the foundation of your roster.

| Heritage | Description | Scaling Interaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard** | Adaptive baseline (Human-coded) | No penalties to switching between Bio or Tech paths. |
| **Primal** | Ancient/Dimensional beings | High affinity for energy and arcane scaling. |
| **Mutated** | Entities warped by environmental corruption | High physical stats; scales with biological instability. |
| **Hybrid** | **Locked:** Requires creation discovery. | High synergy mixtures of Synthetic, Primal, and Arcane paths. |

### The Creation Discovery

Finding your place in B often starts in the gutters with a twitchy, oil-slicked rodent named **Ptouille** who mentors you in the hardware-heavy ways of the scavengers. He explains the technical nuances of the **Rat-person**, showing you how to integrate synthetic hardware without losing your primal edge.

On the opposite side, you find guidance from a neon-coded **Anime Girl** who embodies the high-agility of the **Cat-person**, teaching you to balance arcane energy with primal manifestation. Through their combined lessons, you achieve the **Creation Discovery** needed to manufacture entities that defy the binary.

> Note: We promise this game isn't about being a furry or anime waifus.

---

## Heritage Affinity Groups

**Standard [Adaptive]**
* **Human**: The neutral baseline for multi-pathing.

**Primal [Enduring]**
* **Elven / Fae / Fairy**: High energy pools and resistance to psychological debuffs.

**Corrupted [Mutated]**
* **Goblins**: High scavenging and craft speed.
* **Trolls**: Humans mutated by environmental toxins. High physical power and base regeneration.
* **SuperTroll**: Absolute mutation. Massive health pools and metabolic damage.

**Hybrid [Mixed Integrity]**  -  *Locked until Creation Discovery.*
* **Rat-Person** (Synthetic/Primal): Scavenger-focused with high repair speed and hardware integration.
* **Neon-Liches** (Synthetic/Primal): Practitioners who've gutted their vital organs and replaced them with specialized batteries and cold-iron processors. The trade-off? They need constant high-voltage juice to channel ancient arcane currents - without the tech, they'd burn up from the inside out.
* **Flesh-Weavers** (Mutation/Primal): Monsters that weaponize their own unstable biomass. They sprout tentacles, spines, or webs from their mutating flesh - organic conduits that crackle with raw arcane energy. Unpredictable, volatile, and absolutely devastating.
* **Viral-Chassis** (Mutation/Synthetic): Their immune system got overwritten by a self-evolving firewall. Now their flesh scars over into jagged metallic plating - neither fully flesh nor fully machine. The perfect hybrid: organic adaptability meets machine durability, locked in permanent conflict.
* **Cat-Person** (Primal/Arcane): Agility-focused, with high detection and energy recovery.
