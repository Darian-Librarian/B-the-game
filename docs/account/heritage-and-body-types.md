# Heritage, Body Types, & Affinity

Every player begins their journey through **Character Creation**, selecting the foundational pillars that define their physical and energetic existence.

* **Heritage**: Your origin and soul architecture.
* **Body Type**: Your starting physical state on the **Integrity Axis**.
* **Affinity**: Your primary energy manifestation.

> Your character's biography is yours to define; These pillars simply to help determine how that story translates into your character creation.

---

## Heritage Classifications

Heritage is about your baseline architecture. It is the `Who` behind the `What`.

| Heritage | Description | Synergy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard** | The adaptive baseline. Reliable and flexible | Balanced development |
| **Primal** | Ancient entities from dimensional or mythic origins | Primal and Arcane compatibility |
| **Mutated** | Species forged by environmental toxins or instability | High Biomorphic growth potential |
| **Hybrid** | **Locked:** Requires creation discovery. | Multi-path mastery |

---

### Heritage Affinity Groups
**Standard [Adaptive]**:
* **Human**: The neutral baseline for multi-pathing.

**Primal [Enduring]**:
* **Elven / Fae / Fairy**: High energy pools and resistance to psychological debuffs.

**Corrupted [Mutated]**:
* **Goblins**: High scavenging and craft speed.
* **Trolls**: Humans mutated by environmental toxins. High physical power and base regeneration.
* **Giants / Trolls / Ogres**: Absolute mutation. Massive health pools and metabolic damage.

**Hybrid [Mixed Integrity]**  -  *Locked until Creation Discovery.*
* **Rodent-like** (Synthetic/Primal): Scavenger-focused with high repair speed and hardware integration.
* **Neon-Liches** (Synthetic/Primal): Practitioners who've gutted their vital organs and replaced them with specialized batteries and cold-iron processors. The trade-off? They need constant high-voltage juice to channel ancient arcane currents - without the tech, they'd burn up from the inside out.
* **Flesh-Weavers** (Mutation/Primal): Monsters that weaponize their own unstable biomass. They sprout tentacles, spines, or webs from their mutating flesh - organic conduits that crackle with raw arcane energy. Unpredictable, volatile, and absolutely devastating.
* **Viral-Chassis** (Mutation/Synthetic): Their immune system got overwritten by a self-evolving firewall. Now their flesh scars over into jagged metallic plating - neither fully flesh nor fully machine. The perfect hybrid: organic adaptability meets machine durability, locked in permanent conflict.
* **Feline** (Primal/Arcane): Agility-focused, with high detection and energy recovery.

#### The Creation Discovery

You are initially restricted to standard manifestation paths. To transcend these limits and unlock **Hybrid** species like the **Cyborg-Lich** or **Viral-Chassis**, you must seek out the **Creation Discovery** guides:

* **Ptouille**: Master of synthetic survival and hardware adaptation.
* **The Neon Girl**: Master of arcane synchronization and energy harmony.
