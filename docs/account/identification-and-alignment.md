# Identification & Alignment

The final stage of establishing a player in **B** is the definition of their identity. This step tethers the physical vessel to the world's social and moral systems, determining where you manifest and how the law interacts with your presence.

---

## Player Identification

Every player must be uniquely identifiable within the network. This data is the primary metadata for your **Player Roster** and appears on your character identification card.

* **Unique Name**: Names are unique account-wide and serve as the primary handle for scripts and social interaction.
* **Biography**: This text defines your history, physical description, or operational directives. How you arrived in the simulation - transported by the **Galactic Federation of B** - is entirely up to you to story within this profile.

---

## The Affiliation Cycle

Unlike your Heritage, which is immutable, your **Affiliation** is a dynamic cycle. Your standing shifts based on the paths you take and the actions you perform.

| Affiliation | Description | Scaling Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero** | Operates within established laws to protect the innocent. | **Influence** & Public Support |
| **Villain** | Operates in direct opposition to order (**E.V.I.L.**). | **Resistance** & Black-market access |
| **Vigilante** | Operates outside the law to achieve specific goals. | **Stealth** & Solo Survival |
| **Neutral** | Focused on survival, trade, or personal progression. | Baseline Adaptability |

**The Cycle**: Players can shift between these states over time. A Hero who consistently causes chaos may drift into being seen as a Vigilante or a Villain, while a Villain can seek redemption through specific world events.

---

## Manifestation Sites

Depending on your intent, you will be introduced through one of two starting cities.

#### Atlas City
New players are introduced to the city by the massive Atlas statue.
- **Civilian Beginning**: Any player starting as Neutral may choose this location.
- **Legal Risk**: Atlas City police are significantly more likely to attempt to arrest you if you begin here as a **Hero** or **Vigilante**.

#### Lemon City
The lawless heart of the **E.V.I.L.** movement.
- **Neutral Start**: Any player starting as Neutral may choose this location.
- **Environment**: Despite being a "heavy cop town," Lemon City remains a lawless sector.
- **Social Impact**: Starting here as a **Hero** means the locals mock you and villains have free reign to do whatever they want.
