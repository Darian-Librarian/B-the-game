# Archetypes & Powersets

+ Your Archetype is the main blueprint for how your player interacts with the combat simulation. While your **Heritage** provides the soul and your **Integrity** provides the body, the Archetype defines your role in a party or as a **Sovereign** unit.

---

## The Civilian Path

If you choose to manifest without a specialized blueprint, you're classified as a **Civilian**. This path skips the standard primary and secondary powerset structure at the beginning of your appearance.

* **No Archetype**: Civilians have no primary or secondary focus.
* **Power Selection**: Instead of standard pools, you pick **three powers** from a single powerset of your choice.

---

## Simple Archetypes

These starting blueprints set your primary focus, giving you two of your first power selections in your primary and one in a secondary powerset of your choice.

| Archetype | Primary Focus | Role Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brute** | Melee | High melee damage |
| **Blaster** | Ranged | High ranged damage |
| **Tank** | Defense / Resistance | Avoidance and survival |
| **Team Support** | Support | Team buffs |
| **Controller** | Control | Crowd control and status effect dominance |
| **Hivemind** | Neural / Node | Multitasking via summoned or linked units |

---

## Complex Archetypes

Complex Archetypes have a default secondary focus and receive two primary and two secondary powers from two powersets.

| Archetype | Primary Focus | Secondary Focus | Role Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Super Tank** | Resistance / Defense | Defense / Resistance | Pure mitigation followed by avoidance |
| **Scrapper** | Melee | Defense / Resistance | Balanced melee survival and output |
| **Sentinel** | Ranged | Defense / Resistance | Durable ranged combatant |
| **Dominator** | Control | Melee / Ranged | Direct control with aggressive melee backup |
| **Archon** | Control | Support | Disrupting enemies while bolstering allies |
| **Defender** | Support | Melee / Ranged | Team buffs and healing with ranged utility |
| **Hardline** | Support | Defense / Resistance | Team buffs mixed with defensive focus |
| **Catalyst** | Neural / Node | Support | Multi-unit management with support focus |
| **Tactician** | Neural / Node | Control | Unit management mixed with field control |
| **Enforcer** | Melee | Support / Control | Melee damage that debuffs or heals |
| **Paladin** | Melee | Support | Close quarters combat that buffs the party |
| **Glass Cannon** | Ranged / Melee | Melee / Ranged | Absolute high-damage glass cannon |

---

## Neural/Node Classification: Minions vs. Players

In the **Neural** and **Node** powerset categories, the difference between entities is based on their duration and roster status:

**Total Level**, and remain in the world to perform **AFK** tasks.
* **Minions**: Temporary entities like illusions, pets, or decoys. They don't occupy a player roster slot and disappear after their duration or health expires.
* **Players**: Entities that become a permanent part of your **Player Roster**. They occupy a slot, contribute to your **Total Level**, and remain in the world to perform **AFK** tasks.

## Integrity Influence

Your initial **Integrity** selection on the **Bio-Synthetic Scale** filters your available powersets. All archetypes are eligible, but the specific pools you can draw from depend on your physical composition.

![](/../assets/icons/archetypes/synthetic_filter.png) **Synthetic Integrity (-)**: Leaning toward the **Synthetic Path** unlocks **Industrial** and **Networking** powersets. At 100% Synthetic (Automaton), access to **Ethereal** and **Primal** powersets are entirely disabled.

![](/../assets/icons/archetypes/mutation_filter.png) **Mutation Integrity (+)**: Leaning toward the **Mutation Path** unlocks **Primal** and **Pathogen** powersets. At 100% Mutation (Pathogen), access to higher **Synthetic** powersets are entirely disabled.

![](/../assets/icons/archetypes/purity_filter.png) **Purity (Baseline)**: Players maintaining high **Purity** (near 0% on the axis) have the unique advantage of accessing the widest variety of powersets simultaneously.
