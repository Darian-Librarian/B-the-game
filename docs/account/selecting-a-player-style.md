# Selecting a Player Style

In the **B** logs, you aren't locked into a single identity. You manage a **Player Roster**, allowing you to manifest multiple entities on your account and choose a specific operational style for each. Your style is a dynamic choice that can be changed based on your **Heritage**, **Body Type**, and your player's **Integrity**.

---

<h2>Standard [ The Individual ]</h2>

*| Recommended for new players*

This is the baseline experience where players function as independent entities. You can team up with others or run solo without penalties. Standard players typically originate as **Manifested** (leaning mutation), are created synthetically, or are brought to the city by normal means.

<h3>Inherited Skills</h3>

By following this path, you gain access to these inherited skills. Note that these are subject to shift depending on the current state of your **Integrity**.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/diversity-synergy.png) **Diversity Synergy**: Gains Efficiency and Recovery boosts for every standard player in your party.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/integrity-versatility.png) **Integrity Versatility**: Can maintain moderate levels of both Synthetic and Mutation Integrity simultaneously.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/adaptive-link.png) **Adaptive Link**: Efficiency scales with the proximity of other players in the party. 

---

<h2>Ironman [ The Sovereign ]</h2>

Ironman players reject the collective to maximize their **Sovereign** potential. You become the raid boss of your own story, gaining massive stat multipliers but losing strength when other players interfere with your proximity. You can be affected by other players but can't aid or trade with others.

<h3>Inheritable Skills</h3>

These inherited skills define the Sovereign path, though they are modified by your **Integrity** levels.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/tactical-advantage.png) **Tactical Advantage**: High base defense and damage that increases when enemies surround you.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/self-sustain.png) **Self Sustain**: High Max HP and Energy; regenerative abilities are most effective when solo.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/Unyielding-mind.png) **Unyielding Mind**: Immune to signal interference and status effects because there's no network link to exploit.

---

<h2>Neural [ The Collective ]</h2>

These are the **Neural or Pet type** players - the operators controlling multiple entities like a hivemind pathogen or a coordinated robotic network. You multiply your efficiency through shared systems, mirroring the command structure of **The Galactic Federation of B**.

<h3>Inherited Skills</h3>

These skills are inherited as part of the Collective Will. Their stability is tied directly to your **Integrity**.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/shared-vision.png) **Shared Vision**: If one unit sees an enemy, the entire group detects it regardless of individual line-of-sight.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/distributed-efficiency.png) **Distributed Efficiency**: Efficiency and critical multipliers scale exponentially when units are in close proximity.

![](/../assets/icons/powers/distributed-trauma.png) **Distributed Trauma**: The ability to share incoming damage across the group to prevent unit loss.

---

<h2>Operational Downtime</h2>

Your player will be persistent while you're AFK or when you log off. However, their survivability relies on reaching a safe place whether you are retreating to your apartment, returning to work back at a base, or clocking into a Day Job at a local facility. To ensure best performance during these transitions, the game enforces a recovery cycle:

**Efficiency Decay**: Experience gain starts decreasing after five minutes of repetitive automated tasks.

**Recovery Protocol**: Gain rates return to 100% after nine hours of downtime. This is designed so you can sleep, eat, and actually talk to your wife. **Darian** misses you ever since you got addicted to the grind.
