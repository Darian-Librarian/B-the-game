# Badges

This index tracks the structural milestones and honors earned within the **B** archive. Badges quantify your operational efficiency and standing within the persistent sandbox.

---

## Badge Types

* **<a href="#/badges/achievements" class="stub-link">Achievements</a>**
  - General gameplay milestones and combat tests.
* **<a href="#/badges/accolades" class="stub-link">Accolades</a>**
  - Unique honors awarded for exceptional feats or community recognition.
* **<a href="#/badges/exploration" class="stub-link">Exploration</a>**[cite: 2, 6]
  - Navigation data earned by mapping the sectors of **Atlas City**, **Lemon City**, and beyond.
* **<a href="#/badges/gladiator" class="stub-link">Gladiator</a>**[cite: 2, 6]
  - Performance data tracking both PvP victories and total kill counts.
* **<a href="#/badges/integrity-path" class="stub-link">Integrity Path</a>**
  - Specialized honors based on your standings of **Synthetic**, **Mutation**, and **Purity**.
* **<a href="#/badges/veteran" class="stub-link">Veteran</a>**
  - Recognition of account age and long-term presence in the archive.
* **<a href="#/badges/efficiency" class="stub-link">Efficiency</a>**
  - Technical honors for high-performance scripting, macro uptime, and loop optimization.
* **<a href="#/badges/neural-and-pet" class="stub-link">Neural & Pet</a>**
  - Data for operators managing multiple focus points, minions, or high-level pets.
* **<a href="#/badges/scientific-advancement" class="stub-link">Scientific Advancement</a>**
  - Milestones for the research and development of new skills and high-level technologies.
* **<a href="#/badges/arcane-arts" class="stub-link">Arcane Arts</a>**
  - Mastery of magical systems and complex energy source management.
* **<a href="#/badges/technical-mastery" class="stub-link">Technical Mastery</a>**
  - Exclusive honors for those contributing to the **B** codebase, documentation, or assets.
* **<a href="#/badges/day-jobs" class="stub-link">Day Job Badges</a>**
  - Earned by operators who log off at designated facilities (such as hospitals) instead of a base.
