# B

B is aiming to be a combination of a game focusing on various activity and gameplay styles using a 16-bit and 32-bit low-poly digital aesthetic. This project is a massive overhaul of our previous standard Minecraft experience, utilizing custom Denizen scripts to replace nearly every traditional plugin custom hand-written content.

---

## Project Status: The Meta Phase

**B** is currently in an early, foundational stage of development. The current focus is strictly on **Meta-Documentation** and architectural planning. We are establishing the "Soul of the Game" - defining the mechanics, the lore, and the technical standards - before the any full-scale scripting and development of the gameplay systems.

While the server at `play.behr.dev` exists, the game is far from a finished state. We are currently documenting the vision to ensure that every script and asset added later fits the gritty, high-intensity atmosphere we require.

---

## Large areas of focus

- **Custom Character Creation**: Moving away from generic RPG tropes in favor of a unique system of Heritage, Body Types, and Affinities.
- **The Integrity Axis**: Full-scale body-composition systems where player can define if they are machine, mortal, or a monster.
- **Gritty 90s Aesthetic**: Comic visuals inspired by the high-contrast ink and bold shadows of indie comic legends like Greg Capullo and Todd McFarlane and Pixelated digtal art
- **Efficiency-First Gameplay**: Designed for high-tier solo and group play, supporting multiboxing and automated scripting to maximize operational output.
